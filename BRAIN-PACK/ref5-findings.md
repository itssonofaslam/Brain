## 2. PACING ACROSS 24 MINUTES (ref5-pacing-by-block.md) - the long-form lesson
Mean shot by 4-min block: 2.2 s -> 2.6 -> 2.9 -> **3.5 (jail/trial, slowest)** -> 3.1 -> 2.9. Shots over 6 s: 1, 2, 5, **10**, 3, 1. So a long film does NOT keep the 2 s rhythm of the 7-min explainers: it opens fast (hook + home scene), slows into the emotional middle with long holds and close-ups, then re-tightens for the climax. Brain rule for long-form: pace curve = fast open (2.0-2.4 s), slowing middle (3-3.5 s with 6-10 s holds on emotional beats), re-tightened climax (2.5-3 s), long calm final shot.

4. Callbacks: kitchen set, first-pancake burnt, "Ilu sen", stairs "one step at a time with both hands on rail" = weight of Earth shown by body action (no effects). Payoff plaque flip. The brain should plant a physical prop/phrase early and flip it at the end.
5. Weight device: mother on stairs (0:42-0:48): slow single steps, hands on rail, low camera = gravity shown by posture. In Vael: floating/glide poses, capes, cloud sea below. Lever: posture + camera height.
6. Silent-body acting: extreme close-ups of faces (eyes, tear drops, brows) held 3-8 s carry emotion; no zoom, no shake.
7. Exposition as set dressing: pin-board/plaque wall (Wall of the Fallen), tapestry/banners with spiral emblem, maps/diagrams on the captain's wall, teapot-pour insert of the Hollow gravity rings (macro insert), top-down Hollow funnel shot.

## 5. How ref5 changes the rules
- Darkness is NOT a rule (near-black 4% here vs 63% ref3); mood is set by hue (warm vs cool) and light shape, not blackness.
- Mean shot 2.8 s keeps in range 2.0-3.2; but long-form pace varies 2.2-3.5 s across the film; the brain needs a pace curve, not a constant.
- No CTA end card in a film; ends on "THE END" + fade. CTA is genre-specific (explainers: yes; story films: no).
- Hero in red hoodie again (red in 3/5, blue in 2/5).
- Still: 40-41 px bars, 24 fps, mostly locked camera (88%), no bounce (0/88), cold open, time-tag after cold open, dissolve family 14 f for world/time changes.
