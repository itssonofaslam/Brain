# LABEL CODEBOOK (one code per shot; add flags after a dot)
SIZE/TYPE codes (what the frame mainly shows):
W  wide: characters small (<15% height) in a full set
M  medium: figures about 15-45% of height, full or half body
C  close: head/upper body fills 45-85% of height, still some set visible
X  extreme face close-up: head fills >85% / cropped by frame, eyes+mouth dominate
O  object macro insert (phone, cake, receipt, cup, sign) no face
H  hand/limb close-up (hand action, pointing, pouring, holding)
U  UI/HUD/screen/diagram/map/list/board (graphic content fills frame)
T  text card / title / end card
D  top-down or aerial/establishing without characters or tiny
E  empty environment (no character) establishing/transition plate
B  black/white/flash/transition frame
FLAGS after a dot: p = new place vs previous shot; e = emotional beat (face shows strong feeling or tears); o = over-the-shoulder (foreground blurred head); t = two-shot both figures full; s = speed/action pose; k = crowd/many figures; m = macro of face part (eye)
EXAMPLES: W.p  | X.e | C.o | O | H | U | M.t
