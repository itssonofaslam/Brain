MASTER STYLE BLOCK (ref2 verbatim from dossier p7):
Create a cinematic stylized 3D CGI explainer scene in a coherent minimalist story world. Use true three-dimensional sets, not flat background plates: buildings, sidewalks, tables, doors, cars, sports courts, stadium seating, nets and field markings must have perspective, occlusion, contact shadows and parallax. Keep the overall 16:9 landscape composition. Use a dark indigo / violet / plum nighttime palette with warm amber practical window lights, soft magenta dusk skies, restrained cyan stadium fill and bright white floodlights. Characters have large rounded matte-black heads, very simple high-contrast white graphic eyes/brows/mouth, slender elongated bodies and slim articulated limbs. Facial identity is conveyed by the same eye/mouth geometry and head angle; do not add realistic skin pores, detailed noses, hair strands or photoreal facial anatomy. The main hero has a black round head, sparse white facial marks, red upper garment with dark zip/seam details, slim dark trousers with pale edge piping and dark shoes with light soles; use the red goalkeeper kit and bright yellow gloves only for football goalkeeper scenes. Use smooth simplified materials, subtle specular highlights, clean silhouettes, motivated rim lighting, soft pools of light and deep but readable shadows. Use cinematic depth of field: sharp insect/prop/face inserts with bokeh in the background, wider focus for spatial action and sports geography. Use smooth composed virtual-camera movement (slow dolly/pan, low side-on tracking, overhead/trajectory shots and deliberate wide-to-close cuts), not accidental handheld shake. Use short speed circles, white radial bursts, small particles or motion trails only at specific decisive contacts. Keep the protagonist's head scale, face marks, body proportions and wardrobe logic consistent between scenes. Add crisp readable text/UI only as separate post-production graphics or textures on screens.
