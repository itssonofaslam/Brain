# ANGLE + TRICK CODEBOOK (final, as used in angles_ref1-5.csv)
Only exceptions are tagged; any shot not listed is plain eye-level. Shot code D (top-down) is counted as the D trick automatically.
L low angle (looking up at figure/legs/towers) | V high angle looking down (not full plan view) | D top-down plan view | S scale contrast (tiny figure in vast frame, or one giant element) | F foreground frame (over-the-shoulder, bars, net, railing, bokeh or blurred silhouette in front) | R frame-in-frame (window, porthole, monitor, poster, face-detect box) | P POV or in-world camera (REC, cockpit, flashlight, hands into lens) | K backlit silhouette | Z dutch tilt
(An earlier draft used lowercase codes; those were discarded and the files cleaned.)
